ABOUT THIS DEMO

Kind, but with a plan

Chat

You play the scammer. Yai's replies come from an AI model (OpenAI) told to stay in character and keep you talking. Her opening line is scripted.

Note

Simple pattern matching in your browser spots account numbers, phone numbers, links, LINE IDs, account names and banks, and shows the message each came from. A matching pattern does not mean a real or mule account. Every clue is unverified.

Review

You can review the clues and export them as a JSON file. No money moves, no calls go out, and no report is sent anywhere.

Voice calls use OpenAI Realtime: your microphone audio goes to OpenAI during the call, up to 3 minutes each. The notebook and transcripts stay in this browser (local storage); the page does not save audio.
